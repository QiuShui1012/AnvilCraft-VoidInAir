# Pulp

Soaking <ref item="anvilcraft:wood_fiber"/> in a water cauldron consumes one quarter of a bucket of water and produces <ref item="voidinair:pulp"/>.

<recipe id="voidinair:solid_liquid/pulp"/>

Pulp can be stamped into paper, cooked with resin into slime balls, or compressed with wood fiber and resin into plywood.
