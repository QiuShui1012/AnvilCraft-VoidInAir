# Additional Amulets

These items follow the acquisition and activation rules described on the AnvilCraft [Amulets](../006_prop/100_amulet.md) page. To obtain the
first copy, survive the corresponding fatal damage with a Totem of Undying inside an active Amulet Box. Further copies can then be made at a
Jewelcrafting Table.

| Amulet                                   | Fatal Damage Source                     | Effect                                                                      |
|:-----------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------|:----------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| <ref item="voidinair:void_amulet"/>      | Void damage                             | Immunity to void damage and reusable void rescue                            |
| <ref item="voidinair:snowflake_amulet"/> | Freezing                                | Immunity to freezing and the ability to walk on powder snow                 |
| <ref item="voidinair:beehive_amulet"/>   | Bees                                    | Bees ignore the wearer, allowing honey and honeycomb to be harvested safely |
| <ref item="voidinair:gold_amulet"/>      | Piglins or Piglin Brutes                | Piglins regard the wearer as wearing gold armor                             |
| <ref item="voidinair:dolphin_amulet"/>   | Dolphins, Guardians, or Elder Guardians | Grants Dolphin's Grace and greatly reduces Guardian targeting range         |
| <ref item="voidinair:pumpkin_amulet"/>   | Endermen or Creakings                   | Acts as a virtual carved pumpkin                                            |
